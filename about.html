About us

Built in Durban, for Durban roofs.

A family company that supplies and installs seamless aluminium guttering, and stands behind the product with a 10-year factory guarantee.

Who we are

Alpha Gutters (Pty) Ltd was co-founded by Bashie and Viloshnee Govender. We are a family-run local business specialising in the supply and installation of seamless aluminium guttering, operating throughout Durban and surrounding areas.

We are BEE certified and a registered VAT vendor, with years of experience in the industry. Our aluminium guttering is proudly South African, locally produced, and test-certified by Hulamin. The products are not cheap or fake imports. The minimum factory guarantee of 10 years is how confident we are in the material.
